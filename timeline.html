Pattern Indicators

  • Pre-decision behavior: The hypothetical (“if Brian leaves, would you stay?”) preceded any presentation of reasons. The decision was forming before the case was made.
  • Authority claim inconsistency: Executive involvement referenced once during persuasion; never substantiated, never referenced afterward.
  • Consensus signaling: “Parents are saying” references without verifiable attribution.
  • Financial transparency gap: Across the 2025 two-season reconciliation, $24,400 was collected from families, only $9,000 was actually paid to operations, and $9,000 in field rental remains owed to a documented third-party creditor (Westinghouse Sportsplex). Estimated net retained by Mark: $15,400.
  • Creditor obligation discovered: Two dated, itemized invoices from Westinghouse Sportsplex prove that the practice field provider has not been paid for services families paid for through dues. See Evidence.
  • Retaliation timing: Brian's removal and his daughter's cut land in the same window as the dues waiver, suggesting the question-asker, not the financial structure, was the real problem.
